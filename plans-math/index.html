Balances Equilibrium

plans-math, a Java library of krm-demo/ai-plans-instr · krm-demo.org

A cash sweep keeps the balance x of a current account (a DDA, demand deposit account) between Tmin and Tmax, or at one target balance T, by moving money In from and Out to an investment account. The graph shows the swept amount y for every current balance x. Drag the handles; T switches to one target balance, $ to amounts.

Sweep statuses

  • Swept In Zero ⏚ — a sweep In is needed, but the investment account is empty (A = 0): terminal failure ⏚. Like a yellow card: on the next round the plan is cancelled or paused.
  • Swept In Partial — far below Tmin: all of A comes back and the balance is still short; the investment account is emptied.
  • Swept In Full — exactly the missing Tmin − x comes back from the investment account, at least τmin (at Tmin − A that is all of A: emptied to the cent).
  • In Nominal Min — below Tmin by less than τmin: not worth a transfer.
  • In Range — between Tmin and Tmax (or right at the target T): nothing to transfer.
  • In Nominal Max — above Tmax by less than τmax: not worth a transfer.
  • Swept Out — the excess x − Tmax, at least τmax, goes from the DDA to the investment account; exactly L is still allowed.
  • Swept Out Overflow ⏚ — the excess is above L, the largest single payment the investment account accepts: terminal failure ⏚.

Colours of Swept In

Three softer, coffee-and-cream variants of the two Swept In slices to choose from; everything else stays the same. The choice changes the graph above and the status names (the pictures of the library below keep the current colours).

The same without JavaScript

The library draws one SVG per set of parameters (Java and a Thymeleaf template, no JavaScript inside). The picture adapts to the width it is shown at: the small cart icon, the icon with the $ in the cart, the coloured slices only, then the graph with more and more detail.
